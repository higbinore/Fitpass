# FitPass Elite demo media

These four assets are original SVG illustrations created for the FitPass demo
and are not photographs. They depict an unoccupied gym interior, free-weights
area, cardio floor, and a simple FITPASS ELITE monogram. No external image,
brand, or identifiable person is used; no third-party image license applies.

SVGs are small vector files and remain below the 300 KB target. They are served
from `public/demo/` and do not require Vercel Blob configuration.
